Starling logo variations

OG is your traced original. Versions 1 to 6 are changes to it. Each one is shown big, in the headers, as a browser tab icon, at 32px, on a light background, and in red light.

OG

Your original, traced. The far leg now reaches up under its thigh, so there is no gap.

1. Starling colors

A real starling: dark violet-black with white star speckles. A light purple outline keeps it readable on the night sky, and it gets thicker on the side facing the star, as if the star is lighting the bird.

2. Easy to see from far away

For small sizes like the browser tab: thicker legs and toes, a bigger eye, a bigger star, and one large speckle instead of three small ones that blur together. The tab and 32px icons zoom in on the head, beak and star, since the whole bird is too tall to read at 16px.

3. Nestled

Settled down in a little nest, legs tucked away, still gazing up at the star. Cozy, and the nest gives the logo a solid base.

4. Orion’s Belt

The belly stars become Orion’s Belt: three stars in a straight, even row. A little astronomy joke ("belly" stars, belt stars) for people who notice.

5. Blend

Starling colors and the star-lit outline from 1, the bolder legs, eye and star from 2, and Orion’s Belt from 4.

6. Wildcard: star catcher

The starling balances the star right on the tip of its beak, like it just plucked it out of the sky. Playful and curious, and the star sits closer, so the whole logo is more compact.
