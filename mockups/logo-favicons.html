Favicon options

Four ways to fit the starling into a tiny browser tab, for the OG and both edits. "Whole bird" is the full logo. "Bust" cuts the body off at the bottom so the head, eye, beak and star can be much bigger. The "tile" versions sit on a rounded Starling-blue square, like an app icon, so the shape stays clear on both light and dark browser tabs. Each one is shown big, in light and dark tabs at 16px, at 32px, and as a phone home-screen icon.
