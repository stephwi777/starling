Star flares

Every so often one star swells with light, on top of the quiet twinkle the sky already has. Bloom as you picked it, then two subtler, slower versions. Use “Flare now” to watch one on demand, and slow motion to see the shape.

B · Bloom

The one you picked, unchanged. Quick bright rise, wide halo. 0.7s.

B2 · Softer

Smaller, dimmer halo and the star grows less. Even rise and fall. 1.1s.

B3 · Softest

Half-strength halo and a gentle swell, like a slow breath. 1.5s.

Now in the hourly row
